// How it works

Four steps, all local.

Tabs reads a statement, proposes what looks recurring, and lets you confirm before anything is saved.

01 / 04

Import

Pick a screenshot, a PDF statement, or a whole folder. Apple's Vision OCR and PDFKit pull the text out on-device.

02 / 04

Detect

A heuristic parser groups charges by merchant, clusters them by amount, and surfaces the ones that behave like subscriptions.

03 / 04

Review

You're in the loop. Edit the name, price, and billing cycle of each detected row — and see the statement lines behind it — before saving.

04 / 04

Save & remind

Saved subscriptions land in a local store. Tabs schedules a reminder before each renewal so a charge never surprises you.

// What you can do

Built for the full lifecycle.

From spotting a subscription to cancelling it — without a single charge leaving your device.

Scan a screenshot

Snap your bank app's transactions list and let on-device OCR find the subscriptions in it.

Import PDF statements

One statement or a folder of them. More months means better recurrence detection and inferred cycles.

Add manually

Some subscriptions never hit a statement you have. Add any of them by hand in seconds.

Renewal reminders

A local notification before each renewal — delivered by the OS, scheduled with no server involved.

Cancel, don't lose

Mark a subscription cancelled to drop it from your spend total while keeping its history. Restore it anytime.

See real spend

Every cycle normalized to a monthly figure, so a $120/yr plan and a $10/mo plan compare on the same axis.

// Privacy

Privacy isn't a setting — it's the architecture.

Tabs has no networking layer. That's not a promise to keep your data safe in transit; there is no transit.

✓

No uploads. Screenshots, PDFs, and parsed charges are processed on your device and never sent anywhere.

✓

No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.

✓

No iCloud sync. The local store is configured with CloudKit explicitly disabled.

✓

No analytics, no SDKs. The app contains no URLSession calls at all.

✓

Declared, not just claimed. A privacy manifest ships with the app stating zero tracking and zero collected data types.

// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Tabs connect to my bank?+

No. There's no bank login, no aggregator, no Plaid. You hand Tabs a statement you already have (a screenshot or a PDF), and it reads it locally. Nothing about your accounts is transmitted.

What statements work best?+

Text-based PDF statements from your bank or card issuer work best, especially several months at once — recurrence across months is the strongest signal. Image-only (scanned) PDFs won't have extractable text; use the screenshot path for those.

Will it catch every subscription?+

It's tuned to favor precision over recall — it would rather miss an ambiguous charge than fill your list with groceries and one-off purchases. Anything it misses, you can add manually in a few taps.

Which platforms does it run on?+

iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 and later. The Liquid Glass styling lights up on iOS 26 and falls back to a material surface on earlier versions.
