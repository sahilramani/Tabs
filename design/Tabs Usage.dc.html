// Usage

Every feature, end to end.

From the home screen to cancelling a subscription you forgot about — here's how Tabs works once it's in your hands.

01 — HOME

One number that tells the truth

The home screen leads with your total monthly spend, with every billing cycle normalized to a monthly figure — a $120/yr plan and a $10/mo plan sit on the same axis. Below it, active subscriptions are sorted by how soon they renew, and cancelled ones are tucked into their own section.

  • › Renewal-proximity colors flag what's due soon
  • › Deterministic monogram avatars — no bundled logos
02 — IMPORT

Four ways in, all on-device

Tap the + and pick a path — scan a screenshot, import a PDF, import a whole folder, or add one manually (see 06). Statements are read locally; Vision and PDFKit never touch the network.

Scan a screenshot

Apple's Vision framework OCRs your bank app's transaction list, right on your phone.

Import a PDF statement

PDFKit pulls the text from a bank or card statement — no upload, no network.

Import a folder

Point Tabs at months of statements at once; more history means stronger detection.

03 — REVIEW

Nothing is saved without you

Detection is a proposal, not a decision. Tabs shows each candidate with its detected name, price, and billing cycle — all editable — plus the exact statement lines it matched, so you can see why something was flagged. Duplicates of subscriptions you already track are called out. Deselect anything that isn't a subscription, then save the rest.

Tuned for precision over recall — fewer false positives
04 — MANAGE

Cancel without losing the record

Open any subscription to adjust its price, cycle, or next renewal date. When you're done with one, you have two distinct choices:

CANCEL

Keeps the history, drops it from your spend total, stops the reminder. Fully reversible — restore it anytime and the reminder comes back.

DELETE

Moves the subscription and its detected charges to the Trash — restore it from there, or empty the Trash to remove it for good.

05 — REMINDERS

Never surprised by a charge

Each saved subscription schedules a local notification a few days before it renews — delivered by the OS, with no server involved. Cancel a subscription and its reminder stops; restore it and the reminder returns.

06 — MANUAL ENTRY

Add anything by hand

Some subscriptions never show up on a statement you have — a plan billed to another card, or one you just signed up for. Add it manually with a name, price, and cycle in a few seconds.
